    About the game

    IISER Vithura · Campus Drive

    A drivable, near-real-scale IISER TVM campus game at Vithura, Kerala — take out the GLB shuttle fleet or the open campus buggy across the 2026 mapped road network, playing fields, ponds and forested Western Ghats foothills. One scene unit equals one metre.

    Accuracy

    Footprints and positions are real map geometry. Building heights, floors and roof forms are estimates derived from OSM tags, footprint dimensions and the satellite view; the terrain is a smoothed 30 m elevation model. This is not a survey product and should not be used for navigation or planning.

    Controls

    Drive: WASD or arrow keys · Space handbrake · C camera · 1–7 camera views · H horn · F lights · G high beam · B hazards · R reset · M next mode · Esc exit vehicle.

    Map: drag to orbit, right-drag (or shift-drag) to pan, scroll to zoom. Click a building for details. L labels · T trees · B boundary · Z/X time of day · R overview.

    Multiplayer: the host chooses Create room first and shares the code; friends enter that code and choose Join room. Everyone in the room sees each other's vehicles. Every mode is playable in a room; the scoreboard tracks drivers per mode.

    New driver briefing

    How to play

    You are driving a real-scale IISER Vithura campus shuttle. Pick a vehicle and a mode, then follow the gold route line and green pickup rings.

    1

    Drive

    W/S or arrow keys to accelerate and brake, A/D to steer, Space for the handbrake. Press C to cycle cameras and 1–7 for a specific view.

    2

    Pick up students

    Drive to a stop marked by a glowing green ring, stop inside it and open the doors by waiting. Students walk aboard automatically; the HUD shows the next pickup name, distance and ETA.

    3

    Shuttle 101

    Complete all 8 stops on the active route for a ₹500 loop bonus. Every boarded student earns ₹20. Follow the route colour and the next-stop arrow.

    4

    Other modes

    Time Trial races the 8 stops, Precision Park tests your bay parking, and Buggy Safari runs the hilltop trail. Free Drive and Multiplayer still allow student pickups at any stop.

    5

    Map

    Every pickup point is marked on the minimap. Press N (or double-click the minimap) for the full navigation map with routes, pickup times and live traffic.

    6

    Multiplayer

    The host chooses Create room and shares the code. Friends enter that code and choose Join room. Keep the host's game open; up to 8 drivers can cruise together.

    Quick controls

    H horn · F lights · G high beam · B hazards · R reset · M next mode · N map · Esc exit vehicle.
